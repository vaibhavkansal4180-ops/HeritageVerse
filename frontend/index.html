Preservation Intelligence Framework

HeritageVerse ingests multi-source data to generate objective, transparent Heritage Health Scores (0–100) and actionable decision support:

Citizen Heritage Watch: Crowdsourced photo evidence with automated AI preliminary damage estimation.
Encroachment Monitor: Multi-temporal satellite and drone observation in 100m/300m protected statutory buffer zones.
Multi-Hazard Risk Engine: Automated tracking of flood inundation, seismic vulnerability, and micro-climate AQI corrosion.
AI Preservation Assistant: On-site field inspection checklists and prioritized mitigation recommendations.
