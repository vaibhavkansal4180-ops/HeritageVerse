Early Warning Trigger Matrix & Rule Engine

Alerts are automatically triggered based on real-time preservation thresholds:

Structural Distress:

Triggered when multiple crack widening reports or subsidence are detected.

Tourist Saturation:

Triggered when peak footfall exceeds 120% of designated daily carrying capacity.

Environmental Degradation:

Triggered when AQI > 250, SO2/NO2 exceeds 40 µg/m³, or microclimate humidity > 80%.

Buffer Encroachment:

Triggered upon detection of unapproved masonry or excavation in 100m prohibited zone.
